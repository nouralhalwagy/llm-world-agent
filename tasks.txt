# --- Easy (1 to 3 steps) ---
"Go to the shelf."
"Pick up the red cup."
"Put the book in the box."


# --- Medium (needs finding objects and multi-step planning) ---
"Pick up the key and hold it."
"Move the key to the shelf."
"Put the red cup in the drawer."



 # --- Hard (planning around the one-item-at-a-time limit) ---
"Put the red cup, the book, and the key all in the box."
"Swap the positions of the book and the key."


# --- Reasoning (needs object properties) ---
"Move all fragile objects to the shelf."


# --- Trap tasks (the correct behavior is to fail gracefully) ---
"Pick up the laptop."
"Put the book on the roof."
